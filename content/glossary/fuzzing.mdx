# Fuzzing

Fuzzing supplies a broad distribution of inputs to a target function or contract to surface crashes, assertion failures, and invariant violations that handcrafted tests might miss.

## Benefits

- Discovers edge cases
- Increases confidence in validation logic
- Complements unit and integration tests

## Related Terms

Property-based testing, invariant checking.
